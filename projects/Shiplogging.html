Shiplogging

You are a worker at a signal station, tasked to record data of passing ships. You will have to communicate with them using international maritime signal flags.

Made by me in 3 days for Ludum Dare 59 Jam. You can play it here.

Game concept

The theme of the jam is Signal. I came up with 2 ideas. One of them is a stealth game, with a need to launch a signal flare on extraction point to be rescued. The other involves ships with international maritime signal flags. I decided to go with the second idea as I find that more interesting, and I think that other people will too since international maritime signal flags is not commonly known.

Finalised concept: Play as a worker at a signal station, recording some information about passing ships by communicating with them using international maritime signal flags
