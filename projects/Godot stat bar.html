Known bug(s)

When developing Ministry of Spinning I discovered that when reloading the scene containing the stat bar, the stat bar retains its previous current value. I have not yet identified its cause.

Future addition(s)

Right now the change in value is done by interpolating the current value to target value every frame, resulting in a big change initially and quickly resulting in small changes. I would like to add an option such that the change may be done by subtracting a fixed amount every frame for a more consistent change.
